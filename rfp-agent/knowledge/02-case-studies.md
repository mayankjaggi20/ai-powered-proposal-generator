Client: Milaap (name can be used; public case study)
Region: India, Singapore
Industry: Not-for-profit (online crowdfunding platform for medical, education and personal causes)
Salesforce: Nonprofit Cloud (Nonprofit Success Pack)
Problem: Manual, multi-step employee resignation and off-boarding process; fund deposit and transfer data for partners was not syncing reliably and required manual entry.
What we built: End-to-end off-boarding workflow in NPSP (resignation application, notifications, asset handover, document transfer). A data model for partner fund deposits and transfers, plus file upload that is automatically processed into the system.
Outcomes:
- Off-boarding time reduced 50%, errors down 80%, off-boarding admin tasks down 60%
- Data accuracy improved 95%, manual data entry time down 70%
- Partner update timeliness improved 40%, operational efficiency up 30%
